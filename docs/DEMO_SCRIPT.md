# 5-minute demo script

1. Open the welcome page and explain that the project converts sales history into understandable business metrics.
2. Click **Explore with demo data** to open the dashboard. Mention that all included sample records are synthetic.
3. Point out revenue, units sold, product count and average record value.
4. Scroll to the upload card, select a CSV with the required columns and click **Generate forecast**.
5. Explain the forecast total, month-by-month chart, and MAE/RMSE. Clarify that the forecast is a lightweight planning estimate, not a guarantee.
6. Click **Export sales CSV** and verify the downloaded file opens in Excel.
7. At the bottom, click **Architecture & technical details** and explain the frontend, API, parser, forecast logic and limitations.
